Ghost Patrol — Changes from the Original Design

The finished game keeps the original core design: one level,
five increasingly difficult waves, three ghost types, flashlight
energy management, and automatic weapon upgrades.

Changes made during development:

1. Final boss
A Ghost King boss appears after Wave 5. The player must defeat
it to win. It moves horizontally while descending, has a visible
health bar, and causes game over if it reaches the town.

2. Invisible ghosts
Invisible ghosts can only be seen AND damaged while the
flashlight is active. They remain at one health in every wave
to keep the mechanic manageable.

3. Ghost progression
Wave 1 introduces regular ghosts, Wave 2 adds fast ghosts,
and Wave 3 introduces invisible ghosts. Later waves increase
ghost numbers and shorten spawn intervals. Regular ghosts
also gain health to make stronger weapons useful.

4. Town protection
Each ghost that escapes through the bottom removes one heart.
Contact with the player also removes one heart. The ghost
disappears after causing damage.

5. Interface
Health is displayed as three hearts. A cyan meter displays
flashlight energy. Wave announcements appear briefly before
enemies spawn. Start instructions, pause, and ending screens
explain and support the game flow.

6. Restart behavior
Play Again resets the game and begins Wave 1 directly,
without requiring another click on the title screen.

7. Audio
The game uses event-based sound effects rather than background
music. The weapon-upgrade sound was removed because it felt
unnecessary, while the weapon upgrades remain automatic.

8. Visual effects
Projectile colors, sizes, double shots, and trails communicate
weapon progression. The boss flashes when damaged. Additional
player damage flashes and ghost defeat particles were omitted
to keep the project scope manageable.
